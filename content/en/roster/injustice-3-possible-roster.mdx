## Why the Injustice 3 Possible Roster Is Still a Hot Topic

NetherRealm has not announced Injustice 3 as of this writing. That silence is precisely what keeps the conversation alive. After Injustice 2 launched in 2017, the studio shifted to Mortal Kombat 11 and then Mortal Kombat 1, and each new release reignited the same debate about whether DC's fighting franchise would come back.

The narrative is a big part of it. Injustice 2's story mode closes with two divergent endings, and neither one truly resolves the war between Superman's regime loyalists and Batman's resistance. Player experience and community reports consistently point to that unfinished business as the strongest hook for a sequel.

There's also the roster math. Injustice 2's final playable lineup grew past 30 fighters once all downloadable content arrived, which sets a high bar for any follow-up.

## How NetherRealm Actually Builds a Roster

Understanding the studio's process makes predictions sharper. NetherRealm doesn't pick fighters at random — it builds a launch roster around archetype coverage, story needs, and a DLC pipeline that stretches months past release.

| Design Principle | What It Means in Practice |
|---|---|
| Archetype balance | Every roster needs zoners, rushdown, grapplers, and at least one puppet/stance fighter |
| Hero-to-villain ratio | Typically leans hero-heavy, with villains filling counter-matchup roles |
| Story integration | Story mode chapters usually dictate which characters are mandatory |
| DLC structure | Fighter Packs roll out in waves, mixing requested veterans with shock picks |
| Visual variety | Silhouettes and body types are deliberately varied for readability |

For players who want to follow the trail, a few habits help:

- **Watch official channels first.** Announcements surface through [NetherRealm Studios' official site](https://www.netherrealm.com/) and major showcase events before anywhere else.
- **Track fighting-game tournaments.** Reveal trailers have historically premiered around competitive events.
- **Separate requests from signals.** A character topping a fan poll is not the same as a character being developed.
- **Expect a smaller launch roster than the final one.** Post-launch DLC usually adds a meaningful chunk of the total lineup.

If you're building your own wishlist, score each pick against four questions: Does this character fill an unused archetype? Do they have a story reason to appear? Do they appeal to a broad audience? And do they look distinct on screen? Characters that answer yes to three or more tend to show up.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Injustice 3 Possible Roster

**Has Injustice 3 been officially announced?**
No. As of this writing, NetherRealm Studios has not confirmed a third Injustice game. Everything about an Injustice 3 possible roster is speculation based on precedent, story threads, and community demand rather than official information.

**How large would the roster be at launch?**
The safest assumption is a launch lineup in the mid-to-high twenties, expanding past 30 fighters through post-release content — roughly mirroring how Injustice 2 handled its rollout.

**Will Mortal Kombat characters appear again?**
Historically, yes. Scorpion, Sub-Zero, and Raiden have all crossed over, and NetherRealm has shown no reluctance to repeat the trick. A guest slot or two feels close to inevitable.

**Which newcomers do fans request most?**
Based on player experience and community reports, Beast Boy, Constantine, and Static top most wishlists, followed closely by Zatanna's return and characters boosted by recent DC film and television projects.

The honest takeaway: no one outside the studio knows the real lineup yet. But if you weigh returning pillars, archetype gaps, story obligations, and DC's current media push, the shape of a likely Injustice 3 possible roster becomes a lot clearer — and a lot more fun to argue about.

Nobody outside NetherRealm Studios knows who will throw the first punch in the next DC fighter — which is exactly why **Injustice 3 roster predictions** have become a full-contact sport among fighting game fans. The lineup matters because it decides whether a sequel feels like a genuine evolution or a re-run of the last game.

## Why Injustice 3 Roster Predictions Are Heating Up

*Injustice: Gods Among Us* launched in 2013 with 24 fighters. *Injustice 2* arrived in 2017 with 28 at launch and a long, generous DLC tail. Since then, NetherRealm Studios has shipped *Mortal Kombat 11* and *Mortal Kombat 1*, meaning the team has spent roughly a decade away from DC's caped crowd.

That gap cuts both ways. On one hand, a fresh start means the developers can rebuild the roster from scratch rather than incrementally adding to it. On the other, DC's own media slate has shifted dramatically, introducing new audiences to characters the last two games barely touched. You can read more about the studio's history on [NetherRealm Studios' official site](https://www.netherrealm.com/).

There is also the DLC math to consider. Modern NetherRealm fighters launch lean and grow fat: base roster first, then several packs of three to six fighters. That pattern means a launch lineup is really just the opening act — and it is the part fans always predict worst.

| Injustice: Gods Among Us | 24 | 6 | Scorpion |
| Injustice 2 | 28 | 10 | Sub-Zero, Raiden, Hellboy, TMNT |
| Projected Injustice 3 | 28–32 | 8–12 | 3–4 expected |

## Guest Fighters: Where the Roster Gets Weird

Guest characters are now a NetherRealm signature. *Injustice 2* brought in Sub-Zero, Raiden, Hellboy, and all four Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles as a single slot. *Mortal Kombat 1* leaned even harder into crossovers.

For a third DC outing, the safest bets are characters who already have a comic-book home or a proven relationship with the studio. A Mortal Kombat representative is close to a tradition at this point, and Warner Bros. owns enough properties to keep the well stocked.

## Building a Balanced Roster: Role Distribution

A roster isn't a popularity contest — it's a toolkit. If every fighter is a rushdown specialist, the meta collapses. Based on how *Injustice 2* was structured, a healthy 30-fighter launch would roughly break down like this:

| Role | Target Count | Example Fighters |
|---|---|---|
| Rushdown / mix-up | 8–9 | Harley Quinn, Catwoman, Flash |
| Zoner / keep-away | 6–7 | Green Lantern, Deadshot-type marksman |
| Grappler / big body | 4–5 | Bane, Grundy-type bruisers |
| Stance / puppet | 3–4 | Beast Boy, Plastic Man |
| Utility / counter | 4–5 | Katana, Booster Gold |
| Guest / wildcard | 3–4 | Crossover picks |

Notice how the totals skew toward mobility. That's deliberate — fast, expressive characters are simply more watchable on stream, and NetherRealm designs with the competitive scene in mind.

## How to Judge Predictions Like a Pro

If you want your own **Injustice 3 roster predictions** to age better than the average forum post, apply a few filters before you commit.

- **Check the release cadence.** NetherRealm's rosters are planned years ahead of announcement, so leaks that appear overnight are usually noise.
- **Weigh story mode deaths carefully.** The series resurrects characters constantly; a death scene is a plot device, not a roster cut.
- **Watch the DLC pattern.** A base roster of 28 usually means several packs follow, so "missing" characters may simply be delayed.
- **Separate popularity from design need.** A fighter with no distinct archetype is a hard sell, no matter how loud the fan campaign.
- **Treat datamined files as unverified.** Community reports about placeholder strings have been wrong before and will be wrong again.

| Confidence Tier | What It Means | Typical Characters |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Would bet money on it | Batman, Superman, Wonder Woman |
| Tier 2 | Strong odds | Harley Quinn, Flash, Green Lantern |
| Tier 3 | Coin flip | Supergirl, Black Adam, Aquaman |
| Tier 4 | Long shot | Deep-cut villains, obscure heroes |
| Tier 5 | Pure wishlist | Characters with no modern comic presence |

A useful habit is to sort your personal list into those tiers before you post it. It forces honesty about which picks are genuine reads and which are just favorites.

## FAQ

**Is Injustice 3 confirmed?**
No. NetherRealm Studios has not announced a third Injustice game, and no release window has been confirmed. Every **Injustice 3 roster predictions** list, including this one, is speculative until an official reveal happens.

**Who is most likely to return in Injustice 3?**
Batman, Superman, Wonder Woman, Harley Quinn, and the Flash are the safest bets. They anchor the story, they sell copies, and each one brings a distinct playstyle that would be hard to replace.

**Will Injustice 3 have guest characters?**
Almost certainly. Guest fighters have appeared in every modern NetherRealm release, and a Mortal Kombat crossover is the most likely pick given the studio's own catalog.

**How many fighters should we expect at launch?**
Based on the last two entries, somewhere between 28 and 32 is a reasonable projection, with additional fighters arriving through post-launch packs.

The honest answer to all of this is that the roster will surprise us. NetherRealm has a habit of including two or three characters nobody predicted and cutting one everybody assumed was safe. That unpredictability is part of the fun — and it's why the community will keep arguing about **Injustice 3 roster predictions** right up until the first trailer drops.

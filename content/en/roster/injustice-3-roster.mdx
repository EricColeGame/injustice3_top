## Why the Injustice 3 Roster Still Isn't Official

The Injustice 3 roster is the most requested topic in the DC fighting game community — and the hardest one to answer, because NetherRealm Studios has never officially announced Injustice 3 at all. Every "leaked roster" you have seen is speculation, wishful thinking, or a well-edited thumbnail. It matters anyway: the Injustice 3 roster is the main reason people buy these games, and the character list is what keeps a fighter alive in tournaments and online lobbies for years.

That does not mean there is nothing to work with. NetherRealm has a long, consistent history of how it builds rosters, and the story threads left dangling at the end of *Injustice 2* point in specific directions. Below is a clear-eyed breakdown: what is confirmed, what is plausible, and what the community is simply hoping for.

### Confirmed, Rumored, or Just Wished For?

| Status | What it means | Current situation |
|---|---|---|
| Officially confirmed | Announced by NetherRealm, Warner Bros., or DC | No roster, no release date, no title announcement |
| Credible rumor | Reported by established outlets or reliable industry insiders | Nothing tied specifically to an Injustice sequel |
| Community speculation | Fan wishlists, YouTube concepts, forum threads | Abundant, and the source of most "leaked roster" videos |
| Story-implied | Characters whose arcs demand a resolution | Several, based on how *Injustice 2* ended |

The honest takeaway: treat any numbered "Injustice 3 roster list" as fan content until you see it on NetherRealm's own channels. The studio's official site is the only place a real announcement will land first.

## The Core Returns: Who Almost Certainly Comes Back

Every NetherRealm fighter keeps a spine of characters who are effectively untouchable. Batman, Superman, and Wonder Woman have appeared in both main Injustice games and would be near-impossible to cut — they anchor the story mode. Player experience from both games suggests one rule: if a character carried a major story chapter, they usually return. If they were a one-note DLC addition, they often sit out the next game.

### Why Batman, Superman, and Wonder Woman Are Locked

The Injustice universe is built on the Batman-versus-Superman schism that started when the Joker destroyed Metropolis in the first game's backstory. That conflict is the franchise's identity. A sequel without both halves of it would be a different series entirely.

## How NetherRealm Actually Builds a Roster

Understanding the studio's pattern tells you more than any leak. NetherRealm rosters are assembled around archetypes and story roles, not just popularity.

| Roster slot type | Typical count | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Story leads | 3–5 | Drive the campaign narrative |
| Power archetypes | 6–8 | Brawlers, zoners, grapplers, rushdown |
| Villains | 5–7 | Provide conflict and faction balance |
| Wildcards | 3–5 | Oddball picks that surprise players |
| Guest characters | 2–4 | Cross-brand marketing and novelty |
| DLC additions | 6–9 | Post-launch revenue and longevity |

A launch roster in the high twenties that finishes in the high thirties after DLC is the pattern both Injustice games followed. If a third entry exists, expect a similar shape: a familiar core, a handful of loud newcomers, and a DLC plan that stretches well past launch.

### Archetype Balance Matters More Than Popularity

This is the part fans underestimate. A character can be beloved and still miss the cut if their moveset duplicates an existing fighter. That is why "why isn't X in the game?" almost always has the same answer: someone else already fills that slot.

## Story Clues That Shape the Roster

*Injustice 2* ends with Brainiac defeated and two very different outcomes depending on which ending you accept. Batman's ending imprisons Superman in the Phantom Zone; Superman's ending has him seizing control. Either path creates roster obligations.

| Story thread | Roster implication |
|---|---|
| Superman's fate unresolved | Superman and his Regime loyalists stay central |
| The Justice League rebuilding | Hero-side roster expands |
| Multiverse and alternate Earths established | Opens the door to variant versions of existing fighters |
| Magic and cosmic threats introduced | Clears space for Constantine, Fate, or a Lantern villain |
| Teen Titans largely unexplored | Strong opening for Beast Boy, Raven, or Static |

The multiverse angle is especially useful for a fighting game, because it justifies alternate costumes, echo fighters, and surprise inclusions without breaking continuity.

## Guest Characters and the Crossover Tradition

Injustice has always borrowed from outside DC. The first game brought in Scorpion; *Injustice 2* added Sub-Zero, Raiden, Hellboy, and the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. That precedent makes guest slots one of the safest predictions for any future roster.

| Game | Guest fighters | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| Injustice: Gods Among Us | Scorpion | Mortal Kombat crossover works |
| Injustice 2 | Sub-Zero, Raiden | MK guests became a tradition |
| Injustice 2 | Hellboy, TMNT | Comic and cartoon brands are fair game |
| A future sequel | Unannounced | Expect 2–4 guests, announced late |

The practical advice: do not build your dream roster around guest characters. They are usually revealed close to launch, and they rarely appear in story mode.

## Building Your Own Wishlist: Practical Tips

If you want to speculate intelligently instead of chasing clickbait, use a simple filter.

- **Check story relevance first.** A character with an unresolved arc is far more likely than a popular character with no narrative role.
- **Check archetype gaps.** List the playstyles already covered, then look for holes.
- **Check DLC history.** Characters skipped in the previous game's DLC often surface in the next base roster.
- **Ignore thumbnails.** A rendered image of a character in an Injustice style is not evidence.
- **Watch official channels only.** Announcements come from NetherRealm and DC, not from aggregator accounts.

Community reports also suggest that NetherRealm reads feedback closely — which is precisely why wishlist polls and forum threads are worth participating in, even if they change nothing overnight.

## FAQ

**Has the Injustice 3 roster been confirmed?**
No. As of this writing, NetherRealm Studios has not officially announced Injustice 3, so no fighter has been confirmed for it. Any list claiming otherwise is community speculation or fabricated content.

**Will Mortal Kombat characters appear again?**
Historically, yes — Scorpion appeared in the first game and Sub-Zero and Raiden appeared in *Injustice 2*. Guest slots are a franchise tradition, but they are usually revealed late, so nothing is guaranteed until an official announcement.

**Which characters have never been playable on the main roster?**
Beast Boy, Constantine, and Static are the most frequently requested DC characters who have never been playable in a main Injustice game, despite appearing in comics or mobile spinoffs. They top most community wishlists.

**How will we find out the real Injustice 3 roster?**
Through NetherRealm Studios' official channels or a major gaming showcase. Announcements typically come as a cinematic teaser first, with a full roster rollout following over the months before launch.

Until that day, the smart approach is simple: enjoy the speculation, keep your expectations labeled as speculation, and judge every "leaked roster" by whether it comes from an official source. The Injustice 3 roster will be worth the wait — whenever it actually arrives.

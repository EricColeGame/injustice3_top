## Why the Injustice 3 Rumored Roster Debate Refuses to Die

No official announcement has confirmed Injustice 3. Yet the Injustice 3 rumored roster remains one of the most talked-about topics in the fighting game community, and the reasons are easy to understand. NetherRealm Studios delivered two critically acclaimed DC fighters, then shifted its attention to Mortal Kombat for the better part of a decade. Fans have been filling that silence ever since.

That silence matters because roster speculation is not just wishful thinking. It shapes which characters modders prioritize, which matchups content creators theorycraft, and which names dominate community polls. When a studio eventually does reveal a sequel, the loudest requests often echo what players spent years asking for.

This guide breaks down the Injustice 3 rumored roster the way the community actually discusses it: what is plausible, what is pure wishlist, and how to tell the difference.

## How a Rumored Roster Actually Gets Built

Most roster "leaks" are not leaks at all. They are a mix of legitimate signals, half-remembered interviews, and wishlists that get laundered into rumor over time. Knowing the difference keeps you from getting hyped for a character who was never on the table.

| Source type | What it usually looks like | How much to trust it |
|---|---|---|
| Official trailer or studio post | A teaser, logo, or cast reveal | High |
| Voice actor interview or résumé | Performer mentions recording a role | Medium |
| Datamine of an existing game | Unused strings or placeholder slots | Medium to low |
| Anonymous social media "leaker" | Screenshots, vague lists, no evidence | Low |
| Community wishlist thread | Polls, fan art, YouTube concepts | Not a rumor at all |

A useful habit: whenever you see a roster claim, ask which of those five buckets it came from. If the answer is the last two, treat it as entertainment rather than information. Community reports often turn out to be accurate in hindsight, but only because so many predictions are made that some are bound to land.

## How to Track the Injustice 3 Rumored Roster Without Getting Fooled

If you want to follow this topic seriously, a little discipline goes a long way. Here is a practical routine:

- **Follow official channels first.** Studio and publisher accounts beat aggregators every time.
- **Check the date on any leak.** Old, recycled lists are the most common form of fake rumor.
- **Look for corroboration.** One source repeating itself is not two sources.
- **Watch for impossible claims.** A roster of fifty characters at launch contradicts every prior release.
- **Separate wishlist from reporting.** Community reports are fun; they are not confirmation.

| Red flag | What it usually means |
|---|---|
| "Full roster leaked" with no images | Engagement bait |
| A list that includes every popular character | Fan wishlist repackaged |
| Screenshots with mismatched UI | Photoshop or a mod |
| Claims tied to an unnamed insider | Unverifiable |
| Sudden rush of identical posts | Coordinated clickbait |

None of this means you should ignore rumors. It means you should enjoy them at the right altitude — as entertainment that occasionally, and pleasantly, turns out to be right.

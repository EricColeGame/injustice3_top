## Why the Wait for an Injustice 3 Official Trailer Has Stretched On

NetherRealm has historically alternated between its two flagship franchises rather than running them in parallel. After *Injustice 2* arrived in 2017, the studio pivoted back to *Mortal Kombat*, shipping two mainline entries in the years that followed. That rhythm alone explains a lot: the team does not typically tease its next game while the current one is still receiving support.

There is also a marketing reason. Modern fighting game reveals are rarely just a video anymore. A genuine Injustice 3 official trailer would almost certainly arrive bundled with a pre-order page, a beta signup, a release window, and a platform list — all published simultaneously across first-party channels. Publishers hold that moment until every piece is ready, which is why announcement droughts can last years without meaning the project is dead.

Ed Boon, NetherRealm's creative lead, has a well-documented habit of answering direct questions with jokes, emojis, or deliberate non-answers. Community reports treat nearly every one of those replies as a hint. Sometimes they are. Often they are not. The practical takeaway: treat social media ambiguity as entertainment, not evidence.

### What usually triggers a reveal

## Anatomy of a Reveal Trailer — and How to Spot a Fake

If you have spent any time searching for an Injustice 3 official trailer, you have almost certainly landed on a fan-made concept, a clickbait upload, or a "leak" assembled from reused cinematics. Knowing the anatomy of a real reveal makes the fakes obvious.

| Trailer element | Typical length | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Cold-open cinematic | 30–60 seconds | Tone, art direction, and which characters lead |
| Character reveal beat | 15–30 seconds | The headline fighter and their rival |
| Gameplay tease | 20–45 seconds | Confirms real in-engine footage, not just CGI |
| Logo and release window | 5–10 seconds | The only part that counts as a commitment |
| Post-credits stinger | 5–15 seconds | A hidden character or story hook |

Fake trailers tend to fail the same handful of checks. Run any candidate through this list before you share it:

| Check | Red flag | Where to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Upload source | Random channel, no history | NetherRealm Studios' official site or verified publisher channel |
| Thumbnail | Giant red arrows, all-caps "LEAKED" | Official channels never use leak framing |
| Audio | Reused music from a prior game | Real reveals use new or licensed audio |
| Footage | Recycled *Injustice 2* cinematics | Compare character models and lighting |
| Description | Vague "sources say," no press contact | Legitimate reveals link to a store page |
| Timing | Posted at 3 a.m. with no event tie-in | Announcements follow coordinated beats |

One more tell: real trailers are boring about credits. They name the developer, the publisher, and the platform logos. Fan edits almost never bother, because the goal is a view, not accuracy.

## FAQ

**Has an Injustice 3 official trailer been released yet?**
No. At the time of writing, NetherRealm Studios and its publisher have not published any trailer, teaser, or store page for the game. Anything you see labeled as a leaked reveal is unverified at best and fan-made at worst.

**When should we expect an Injustice 3 official trailer?**
There is no confirmed date or window. Historically, the studio announces projects only when they are far enough along to show real footage, and those announcements have been tied to major industry events. Following official channels is the only reliable way to know.

**Why do so many fake Injustice 3 official trailer videos exist?**
Search demand is high and supply is zero, which is exactly the gap clickbait fills. These videos typically reuse *Injustice 2* cinematics, add dramatic music, and use a leak-style thumbnail. Checking the upload source against official channels debunks them in seconds.

**What should I look for to know a reveal is real?**
Four things: an upload from a verified official channel, a simultaneous press release or store page, a release window rather than just a logo, and platform information. A genuine Injustice 3 official trailer almost always ships with all four at once.

**Is Injustice 3 even happening?**
Nobody outside the studio has confirmed it. The series has never been publicly canceled, the studio continues to make DC fighting games, and demand remains high — but interest is not the same as an announcement. Until you see it on an official channel, treat every claim as speculation.

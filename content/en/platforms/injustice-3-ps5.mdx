Few unannounced sequels inspire as much stubborn hope as Injustice 3. Every NetherRealm Studios livestream, every cryptic Ed Boon post, and every Warner Bros. earnings call sends fans back to the same question: is Injustice 3 PS5 actually happening? That question matters because Injustice 2 arrived in 2017 on hardware that predates the current console generation, and a true Injustice 3 PS5 release would be the first entry in the series built from the ground up for modern PlayStation hardware.

This guide separates what is genuinely known from what is wishful thinking, walks through the series' PlayStation history, and shows you how to scratch the Injustice itch on PS5 right now while you wait for an announcement that may or may not come.

## Is Injustice 3 PS5 Officially Confirmed? What We Know

Short answer: no.

As of this writing, neither NetherRealm Studios nor Warner Bros. Games has publicly announced Injustice 3, let alone confirmed it for PlayStation 5. There is no official trailer, no store listing, no release window, and no verified developer statement. Anything you see claiming a "confirmed" date is speculation, a placeholder listing, or an outright fake.

That said, the idea is far from dead. NetherRealm has a long-documented habit of alternating between the Mortal Kombat and Injustice franchises rather than abandoning either one. The studio shipped Mortal Kombat 1 in 2023 and has since moved through its usual cycle of post-launch support followed by work on an unannounced project. Historically, that is exactly the window in which new reveals have appeared.

A practical tip: before you believe any headline, check whether it traces back to an official NetherRealm or Warner Bros. channel. ## The Injustice Series on PlayStation: A Platform Timeline

Injustice has always been a showcase for whatever PlayStation hardware was current at the time. The original arrived near the end of the PS3 era and was quickly re-released for PS4, while Injustice 2 became one of the definitive PS4 fighting games of its generation.

| Title | Release year | PlayStation platforms | What it brought to the table |
|---|---|---|---|
| Injustice: Gods Among Us | 2013 | PS3, PS Vita, PS4 (Ultimate Edition) | Cinematic story mode in a fighting game |
| Injustice: Gods Among Us Ultimate Edition | 2013 | PS4, PS Vita | Cross-gen release with all DLC included |
| Injustice 2 | 2017 | PS4 | Gear system, loot, long post-launch support |
| Injustice 2 Legendary Edition | 2018 | PS4 | Bundled DLC characters and cosmetics |

The gap since Injustice 2 is now the longest the franchise has ever gone without a new mainline entry, which is a big reason why searches for "injustice 3 ps5" keep climbing whenever the studio goes quiet. Fans read silence as a signal, even when it is usually just the normal rhythm of game development.

It is also worth remembering that Injustice's story extends well beyond the games. The tie-in comic series and the 2021 animated film both expanded the universe, and any future game would likely draw on that broader mythology.

## What Injustice 3 Could Do With PS5 Hardware

If an Injustice 3 PS5 version does arrive, the hardware itself would shape the experience in ways the PS4 generation simply could not. Fighting games live and die on responsiveness, and PS5's architecture is built around exactly that.

| PS5 feature | How it could be used | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Ultra-fast SSD | Near-instant rematches and stage loads | Less downtime between matches |
| DualSense haptics | Impact feedback per hit and block | Makes trades and punishes feel physical |
| Adaptive triggers | Trigger resistance for special moves | Adds tactile depth to inputs |
| Tempest 3D AudioTech | Directional crowd and hit audio | Better spatial awareness in arenas |
| 4K at 120Hz with VRR | High-frame-rate competitive mode | Lower latency, smoother motion |
| Ray tracing | Dynamic lighting on destructible stages | More dramatic super move cinematics |

Two features are arguably more important than any visual upgrade: rollback netcode and crossplay. Modern fighting games have made both close to an industry standard, and community reports consistently rank them as the top requests for any new NetherRealm fighter. Rollback netcode makes online matches feel closer to local play, while crossplay keeps matchmaking pools healthy long after launch — a genuine concern for a game that could arrive late in the PS5's life cycle.

A mid-generation console refresh would only add headroom, potentially allowing a higher-resolution mode alongside a performance mode without forcing players to choose.

## How to Play Injustice on PS5 Today

You do not have to wait for an announcement. PS5 backward compatibility means both mainline Injustice games are playable on current hardware.

| Option | What you get | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Injustice 2 (PS4 disc) | Full game and campaign | Runs via backward compatibility |
| Injustice 2 digital | Same content, no disc drive needed | Watch for PlayStation Store sales |
| Injustice 2 Legendary Edition | Base game plus DLC characters and skins | Usually the best value |
| Injustice: Gods Among Us Ultimate Edition | 2013 original plus all DLC | Older netcode; best for local play |
| PlayStation Plus catalog | Rotating availability | Lineup changes, so check before buying |

A few practical tips to get the most out of either game on PS5:

- **Pick the right display mode.** Both titles offered performance-oriented modes on PS4 Pro, and the extra headroom on PS5 generally smooths out frame pacing in those modes.
- **Play online wired.** A wired connection reduces the input delay that makes fighting games feel sluggish.
- **Expect a smaller online pool.** Community reports suggest matchmaking in older NetherRealm titles takes longer outside peak hours, so patience helps.
- **Check Game Boost behavior per title.** PS5's Game Boost can improve performance in some backward-compatible games, but results vary from title to title.
- **Don't confuse the mobile game with the console version.** Injustice 2 Mobile is a completely different product with its own progression system.

It is also worth noting what players do *not* want: an always-online requirement for single-player content, aggressive monetization on cosmetics, or a roster padded with guest characters at the expense of core DC fighters. Those concerns come up repeatedly in community discussions, and they are reasonable signals for what a well-received sequel would need to avoid.

## FAQ

**Is Injustice 3 PS5 officially confirmed?**
No. There has been no official announcement of Injustice 3 from NetherRealm Studios or Warner Bros. Games, and no release date, platform list, or trailer exists. Any claim otherwise should be treated as a rumor until it comes from an official source.

**Can I play Injustice 2 on PS5?**
Yes. Injustice 2 is a PS4 title, and the PS5 plays PS4 games through backward compatibility. Both the standard and Legendary Editions work, whether you own a disc or buy digitally. Injustice: Gods Among Us Ultimate Edition is also playable the same way.

**Will Injustice 3 come to PS5 or a future console?**
That is unknown, because the game itself is unannounced. If a sequel is in development, PS5 would be the natural home given its install base, though a release late in the generation could also mean a cross-generation launch. Speculation about specific dates or platforms is not supported by anything official.

**Where should I follow news about Injustice 3 PS5?**
Stick to official channels first. The [official NetherRealm Studios site](https://www.netherrealm.com/) and Warner Bros. Games announcements are the only sources that can confirm a project, while established gaming outlets are the next best option for verified reporting. Community forums are great for discussion, but they are not confirmation — and a surprising number of "leaks" turn out to be wishful thinking dressed up as fact.

Until something official lands, the smartest move is simple: keep playing the games you already own, keep your expectations calibrated, and enjoy the speculation for what it is. If Injustice 3 PS5 ever becomes real, you will hear about it from the studio — not from a random post with an unverified screenshot.

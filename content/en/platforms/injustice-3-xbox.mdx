Search for **Injustice 3 Xbox** on any social platform and you will find a mix of genuine discussion and confident-sounding fabrication. The pattern is predictable: an unverified "leak" appears, gets reposted without attribution, and within a week is treated as fact.

Here is a simple filter you can apply to any claim:

| Claim Type | Trust Level | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Official trailer from publisher | Confirmed | Direct from source |
| Rating board listing | Strong signal | Regulatory filing |
| Statement from studio employee | Moderate | Could be misquoted |
| Anonymous forum "leak" | Very low | No accountability |
| AI-generated "news" article | Ignore | Often fully fabricated |

Community reports are useful for one thing: gauging demand. They are not useful for establishing facts. If you want a reliable signal, wait for a trailer, a press release, or a store page. Everything else is noise, no matter how detailed it sounds.

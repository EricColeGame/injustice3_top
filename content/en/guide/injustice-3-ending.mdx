## Is There an Injustice 3 Ending Yet? The Short Answer

If you're searching for the **Injustice 3 ending**, you've probably finished Injustice 2, watched both of its finales, and wondered which one actually counts. The honest answer is that no **Injustice 3 ending** exists yet — no game has been released, and no official announcement has surfaced. That doesn't mean the conversation is dead, though.

Everything fans discuss about a potential Injustice 3 ending comes from three places: the dual conclusions of Injustice 2, the tie-in comics that filled in the gaps, and years of community speculation. This guide separates what's confirmed from what's wishful thinking, so you can follow the story threads without getting lost in rumor.

One ground rule before we start: nothing below is presented as leaked or official unless it actually is. Where the community is driving the conversation, it's labeled as player experience or community reports.

The pattern matters. NetherRealm Studios built this franchise on the idea that the player's final fight decides the canon — which is exactly why an Injustice 3 ending is so hard to predict.

## How Injustice 2 Set Up the Next Chapter

Injustice 2 ends with a choice, not a conclusion. Beat the story mode one way and you get Batman's version of events; beat it the other way and Superman writes the final page. Both are "real" until a sequel decides otherwise.

The key point for anyone tracking the Injustice 3 ending debate is that neither finale closes the story. Both leave a major piece of the board untouched, and both leave the relationship between Batman and Superman unresolved in a way that a sequel would have to address in its opening act.

### The Two Injustice 2 Endings Compared

| Element | Batman's Ending | Superman's Ending |
|---|---|---|
| Final confrontation | Batman defeats Superman | Superman defeats Brainiac |
| Superman's fate | Imprisoned and stripped of power | Free, and claiming control |
| Batman's fate | Leads a rebuilt Justice League | Captured and contained |
| Brainiac's collection | Left intact and unclaimed | Claimed as a tool of order |
| Tone of the final scene | Uneasy peace | Escalating regime |
| Setup for a sequel | A fragile, watched world | A world under one fist |

Community reports often frame this as "the Batman ending is the good one," but that's a value judgment, not a fact. What's objectively true is that the Superman ending creates a more aggressive status quo — the kind of status quo a fighting game sequel can escalate quickly.

### Where the Comics Fit

The Injustice 2 prequel comics expanded the Regime's collapse and gave supporting characters far more screen time than the game did. Injustice: Year Zero later jumped backward to fill in pre-game history. If you want the fullest picture of what an Injustice 3 ending might resolve, the comics are not optional reading — they're where several dangling threads were first planted.

## The Most Popular Injustice 3 Ending Theories

Fan theorizing has produced a handful of recurring ideas. None are confirmed, and all should be treated as community reports rather than information. That said, some are more grounded in established story logic than others.

| Theory | Core Claim | Supporting Logic | Plausibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Batman ending is canon | The sequel continues from Batman's win | It's the more hopeful status quo, easier to build on | Moderate |

The multiverse theory gets the most traction because it solves a structural problem. If both endings are canon on different Earths, then an Injustice 3 ending could deliver a definitive conclusion while still honoring every player's playthrough. It's elegant, it's consistent with the franchise's own rules, and it requires no retcons.

Player experience also plays a role here. Many longtime fans say the emotional payoff of the Superman ending is stronger precisely because it's uncomfortable, while others argue the Batman ending respects the characters more. That split is exactly why the developers have never been forced to pick a side.

## Why the Wait Makes the Ending Harder to Predict

NetherRealm Studios has historically alternated between its two flagship fighting franchises, which means every new release cycle reignites the Injustice 3 ending conversation. When a Mortal Kombat title arrives, Injustice speculation quiets down. When the studio goes quiet, the theories surge again.

That rhythm creates a peculiar problem. The longer the gap, the more the community builds up expectations that no single ending can satisfy. If a finale ever arrives, it will be measured against years of accumulated headcanon — not just against the previous game.

| Signal | What It Suggests | How Much Weight to Give It |
|---|---|---|
| Studio alternating franchises | A DC project may follow a Mortal Kombat cycle | Reasonable pattern, not a promise |
| Unannounced DC projects in reports | Something may be in development | Unconfirmed, treat as rumor |
| Comics continuing the world | The IP remains active | Strong indicator of interest |
| No official trailer or date | Nothing is imminent | Fact |

The healthiest approach is to enjoy the speculation without treating any of it as a schedule. The franchise's own publisher and developer are the only sources that matter when an announcement eventually comes.

### What a Finale Would Probably Look Like

If you want to sanity-check any theory you read online, ask whether it matches the franchise's established structure. A credible Injustice 3 ending would likely include:

- A story mode split into character chapters, each ending on a cliffhanger
- A final choice between two outcomes, mirroring the previous two games
- At least one returning hero framed as the emotional center
- A closing scene that either seals the world or deliberately reopens it
- Post-story content that keeps the roster playable regardless of your choice

Any "leak" that ignores this structure is almost certainly fabricated.

## FAQ

**Will there ever be an Injustice 3 ending?**
No official confirmation exists. The franchise remains commercially and creatively active through comics and re-releases, so a continuation is plausible, but nothing has been announced. Until a game ships, every Injustice 3 ending you read about is speculation.

**Which Injustice 2 ending is canon?**
Neither has been officially confirmed as the canon branch. Both are presented as valid outcomes, and no sequel has been released to settle the question. Treat claims of a confirmed canon ending as community opinion.

**Do I need to read the comics to understand the story?**
Not strictly, but they help. The prequel comics flesh out the Regime's rise and fall and plant several threads that a future finale would likely address. Reading them makes the speculation far easier to follow.

**Why do fans care so much about the ending?**
Because the series is built on a single question — can absolute power ever be justified? A final chapter would have to answer it. That's a much bigger deal than who wins a fight, which is why the debate never really stops.

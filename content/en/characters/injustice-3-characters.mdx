Ask any fighting game fan what they want next from NetherRealm Studios and you will usually get the same answer: more DC. The **Injustice 3 characters** roster is one of the most speculated topics in the genre, and for good reason. Injustice 2 ended on a genuine cliffhanger, the tie-in comics kept expanding the world long after the credits rolled, and the series has a proven habit of turning B-list heroes into mainstays.

Any serious conversation about **Injustice 3 characters** has to start with a disclaimer, though: NetherRealm and Warner Bros. have not officially announced the game. Everything below is built from story threads, community demand, and the design patterns the studio has followed across two Injustice titles and multiple Mortal Kombat entries. Treat it as informed speculation rather than a leaked roster.

## Why the Injustice 3 Characters Debate Never Dies

The Injustice series does something most licensed fighters never manage: it makes its roster feel like a story rather than a checklist. Injustice: Gods Among Us introduced a regime-versus-insurgency premise that justified heroes fighting each other. Injustice 2 doubled down with the Society, Brainiac's invasion, and a gear system that let players personalize every fighter.

That structure creates unusually high stakes for roster picks. A character added to Injustice is a character who gets a canonical role in a universe where Superman can become a tyrant and Harley Quinn can become a resistance leader. Players are not just choosing a moveset — they are choosing whose version of the story they want to see.

## Rumored Returning Characters: The Justice League Core

If Injustice 3 happens, a handful of names are effectively guaranteed. Batman, Superman, Wonder Woman, The Flash, and Harley Quinn have anchored both prior games and remain the commercial faces of the franchise. Green Lantern, Aquaman, and Cyborg are close behind — all three have established roles in the Injustice timeline and clear gameplay identities.

Community reports consistently place these characters at the top of "must return" polls, and it is easy to see why. Removing Batman or Superman from an Injustice roster would be like removing Scorpion from Mortal Kombat: technically possible, commercially unthinkable.

### What the Injustice 2 Endings Suggest

Injustice 2 shipped with two endings, and both leave room for a sequel. In the Batman path, Superman is depowered and imprisoned. In the Superman path, the Man of Steel takes control and Batman is forced underground. Either branch sets up a fractured world where new alliances — and therefore new fighters — make sense.

The Injustice comics pushed even further, folding in cosmic threats and alternate-Earth politics. That history points toward a roster that mixes street-level Gotham operatives with cosmic heavyweights.

## Guest Characters and Crossover Potential

Injustice 2 set a high bar for guests, bringing in Mortal Kombat's Scorpion, Sub-Zero, and Raiden, plus Hellboy and the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. That precedent makes guest slots one of the most exciting unknowns for any future installment.

The most common community theory is that a future Injustice could deepen the Mortal Kombat crossover, since both franchises now share a studio and a publisher. Beyond that, anything is possible — the studio has shown a willingness to pull from comics, film, and animation.

## Faction Themes, Playstyles, and Roster Balance

A good roster is not just a list of names — it is a set of answers to the question "how do you like to play?" NetherRealm has historically balanced Injustice rosters by archetype, making sure every major playstyle has at least one strong representative.

If you are thinking about which Injustice 3 characters would actually be fun to main, it helps to sort them by archetype rather than by popularity.

| Archetype | What It Rewards | Past Examples | Ideal Newcomer Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rushdown | Aggression and frame traps | Flash, Supergirl | Beast Boy |
| Zoner | Spacing and projectiles | Doctor Fate, Deadshot | Zatanna |
| Grappler | Reads and command grabs | Bane, Atrocitus | King Shark |
| Stance / hybrid | Adaptability | Blue Beetle, Robin | Constantine |
| Counter / punish | Patience and whiff punishing | Deathstroke, Batgirl | Lobo |

Faction identity matters too. Splitting a roster across the Justice League, the Justice League Dark, the Suicide Squad, the Titans, and the New Gods gives developers natural story groupings and gives players a reason to care about matchups beyond tier lists.

- **Justice League:** the recognizable core that sells the game
- **Justice League Dark:** magic users who fill the roster's spookiest corner
- **Suicide Squad:** villains and antiheroes with grit and gadgets
- **Teen Titans:** younger, faster, more experimental movesets
- **New Gods:** cosmic scale for endgame story beats

## Final Thoughts on Building the Ideal Roster

The safest prediction is that Injustice 3 characters would follow the same formula that made Injustice 2 a success: a recognizable Justice League spine, a handful of deep cuts, at least one magic-focused newcomer, and guest fighters saved for post-launch reveals. For more context on the studio behind the series, the [official NetherRealm Studios site](https://www.netherrealm.com/) is the best place to watch for announcements.

Until something official lands, roster talk is exactly that — talk. But it is productive talk, because it tells developers what the audience actually wants. If you are building your own dream list, prioritize fighters who add a new playstyle rather than a fifth version of a character you already main. That is the difference between a wish list and a genuinely great roster.

## FAQ

**Has Injustice 3 been officially announced?**
Not as of this writing. Neither NetherRealm Studios nor Warner Bros. has confirmed the game, which means every discussion of Injustice 3 characters is based on speculation, story threads, and community demand rather than official information.

**Which characters are most likely to return?**
Batman, Superman, Wonder Woman, Harley Quinn, and The Flash are the safest bets. They have anchored both previous games, appear in the comics, and carry the franchise's marketing weight.

**Will Mortal Kombat fighters appear as guests?**
It is a strong possibility. Injustice 2 already featured Scorpion, Sub-Zero, and Raiden as DLC, and the crossover proved popular with players. A future installment could easily expand that relationship.

**What makes a good newcomer pick?**
A good addition fills a gap rather than duplicating an existing fighter. Look for characters who bring a new archetype, a new faction, or a new visual language — that is what keeps a roster fresh after dozens of hours of play.

## Why the Injustice 3 New Characters Conversation Refuses to Die

Every few months, a blurry screenshot, a deleted tweet, or an offhand remark from a voice actor sends the fighting game community down the same rabbit hole: who are the Injustice 3 new characters going to be? NetherRealm Studios' DC fighting series has been quiet long enough that speculation has effectively become its own subculture, complete with roster mockups, wish-list videos, and "leaked" character-select screens that circulate endlessly.

That's exactly why Injustice 3 new characters remain one of the most-searched topics in the fandom. A sequel's roster is the single biggest promise a fighting game makes, and Injustice has a track record of eventually delivering on names players begged for years in advance. This guide breaks down the realistic candidates, the design patterns NetherRealm follows, and how to separate a credible rumor from wishful thinking.

## What NetherRealm's Roster History Tells Us

NetherRealm doesn't build rosters randomly. Each game blends a core of A-listers, a handful of deep-cut comic picks, and one or two crossover guests. Understanding that formula is the fastest way to predict which names have a real shot.

| Game | Launch roster (approx.) | Notable DLC additions | Guest fighters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Injustice: Gods Among Us | 24 | Lobo, Batgirl, Scorpion, General Zod | Scorpion (Mortal Kombat) |
| Injustice 2 | 28 including preorder bonus | Red Hood, Starfire, Sub-Zero, Black Manta, Raiden, Hellboy, Atom, Enchantress, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles | Sub-Zero, Raiden, Hellboy, TMNT |

A few takeaways jump out immediately:

- **Launch rosters hover around two dozen fighters.** Anyone expecting a 40-character base roster is setting themselves up for disappointment.
- **DLC does the heavy lifting.** Roughly a third of each game's final roster arrived after launch, spread across several fighter packs over 12–18 months.
- **Every cycle includes at least one non-DC guest.** That slot is almost never a DC character, which matters when you're building a prediction list.
- **Comic and screen synergy drives picks.** Roster choices often line up with whatever DC is pushing in comics, animation, or film at the time.

| Pattern | What it means for a potential sequel |
|---|---|
| Sequel rewards long-running fan requests | Characters demanded for a decade become priorities |
| Trait-based movesets define each fighter | Newcomers need a hook, not just a costume |
| DLC extends the roster for a year-plus | A "small" launch roster still grows significantly |
| One or two guests per cycle | Expect at least one outside franchise |

## The Most Requested Injustice 3 New Characters

Community wish lists converge on a surprisingly consistent group. These are the names that show up in nearly every mock roster, ranked here by how often they surface and how naturally they'd fit the game's systems.

Two design realities shape which of these actually make it. First, **shape-shifters and summoners are expensive** — every form needs its own animation set, so a character like Beast Boy effectively costs as much as two or three standard fighters. Second, **characters without an obvious "trait"** tend to get reworked or shelved, because Injustice's core gimmick is that every fighter has a unique character power.

If you're building your own wish list, weigh it the way a designer would: does this character bring a mechanic no one else has?

## Guest Fighters: Injustice's Wild Card Slot

Guest characters are the most debated part of any Injustice roster, and also the easiest to predict in broad strokes. Mortal Kombat's recent guest strategy — pulling in comic-book antiheroes, horror icons, and action-movie legends — shows how far NetherRealm is willing to reach.

| Guest archetype | Precedent | Likelihood if a sequel happens |
|---|---|---|
| Mortal Kombat crossover | Scorpion in the first game; Sub-Zero and Raiden in the sequel | Near-certain |
| Comic-book outsiders | Hellboy in Injustice 2 | Strong, given Warner's comic catalog |
| 80s/90s action icons | Mortal Kombat's guest approach | Plausible, though the tone is trickier |
| TV and streaming stars | Cross-promotion with current Warner projects | Depends entirely on the release window |

The safe bet is that a third game includes at least one Mortal Kombat representative. The community-sourced debate usually centers on whether that slot is "wasted" on a crossover when a DC deep cut could take its place — a fair argument, though history suggests guests sell exceptionally well.

## How a New Fighter Actually Gets Built

Roster speculation gets more useful when you think in archetypes rather than names. Every Injustice fighter lands somewhere on a spectrum, and newcomers are usually chosen to fill a gap in the existing cast.

| Archetype | How it plays | Characters who fit |
|---|---|---|
| Zoner | Controls space with projectiles and traps | Larfleeze, Saint Walker |
| Rushdown | Fast normals, constant pressure, mix-ups | Beast Boy, Static |
| Grappler | Command grabs and punishing damage | King Shark, Kilowog |
| Stance / swarm | Transforms or summons mid-match | Animal Man, Etrigan |
| Puppet / setup | Places objects and assists on screen | Constantine, Raven |

A practical tip: when you see a fan roster, check whether it fills these five roles. Mock rosters stacked with six zoners and no grappler are a tell that the list was built on popularity rather than design sense. Community reports on forums often flag this, and the more thoughtful wish lists always balance the spread.

## Reading the Rumors: How to Tell Signal From Noise

The Injustice 3 new characters rumor mill runs hot year-round, which makes source quality everything. Here's a rough hierarchy based on how reliable each type of "evidence" has historically been.

| Evidence type | Reliability | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Official trailer or press release | Definitive | The only thing that confirms anything |
| Developer or publisher statements | High | Often deliberately vague |
| Voice actor social posts | Medium | Frequently misread; contracts restrict disclosure |
| Datamines of unrelated builds | Low to medium | Placeholder text and reused assets are common |
| "Leaked" character select screens | Low | Trivial to fake convincingly |
| Community reports / player experience | Low | Fun to read, but treat as speculation |

One pattern worth internalizing: rumors about Injustice 3 new characters tend to snowball from a single unverified post into a dozen articles within days. If a claim can't be traced back to a primary source, it's a wish, not a leak.

## Tips for Following Injustice 3 New Characters News Without Burning Out

- **Follow official channels first.** NetherRealm's own announcements and the [official NetherRealm Studios site](https://www.netherrealm.com/) are the only sources that settle a debate.
- **Watch the event calendar.** Reveals historically cluster around major trade shows, fighting-game tournaments, and year-end gaming showcases.
- **Check the date on everything.** Recycled rumors from previous years get reposted constantly with fresh timestamps.
- **Cross-reference at least two outlets.** If only one site is reporting it, wait a week.
- **Keep expectations modular.** Expect roughly 24–28 fighters at launch, with 8–10 added through DLC.
- **Track DC's publishing slate.** Roster picks frequently follow whatever characters DC is pushing in comics and animation.

## FAQ

**Has Injustice 3 been announced?**
As of this writing, there has been no official announcement of Injustice 3 from NetherRealm Studios or Warner Bros. Everything circulating about the game is speculation, rumor, or fan-made concept work.

**Which Injustice 3 new characters are confirmed?**
None. No character has been officially confirmed for a third Injustice game, so any list claiming a verified roster is presenting guesswork as fact.

**Will Injustice 3 include guest fighters?**
Based on the pattern of the first two games and NetherRealm's broader guest-character strategy, at least one non-DC fighter is likely if a sequel happens. A Mortal Kombat crossover remains the safest prediction.

**How many new characters should fans expect?**
If history holds, expect a launch roster in the mid-to-high twenties, with roughly a third of the final roster arriving later as downloadable content. That's the range Injustice 3 new characters would realistically land in — not the 50-fighter dream rosters that dominate social media.

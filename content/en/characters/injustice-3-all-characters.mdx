## Why the Injustice 3 Roster Is Still a Mystery

Ask ten DC fans to list the Injustice 3 all characters lineup and you will get ten completely different answers. That is not a sign of confusion — it is a sign of how much people care about this franchise. Since *Injustice: Gods Among Us* launched in 2013 and *Injustice 2* followed in 2017, NetherRealm Studios has kept the series on ice while it focused on Mortal Kombat. That gap has turned the Injustice 3 all characters question into one of the most debated topics in the fighting game community.

Why does it matter so much? A roster is not just a checklist. It decides how the story mode is structured, which archetypes are available at launch, how balanced the competitive scene feels, and whether casual players buy the game on day one. If you are trying to plan a purchase, a fan project, or just win an argument with your friends, you need to know what is real and what is wishful thinking.

Here is the honest starting point: as of this writing, Warner Bros. and NetherRealm have not officially announced Injustice 3. Any list you see online is speculation, a community wishlist, or a rumor that should be treated with caution. This guide separates the plausible from the pure fantasy and gives you a framework for building a roster that actually works.

## Sorting the Rumors: What Is Actually Confirmed

The biggest mistake fans make is treating a leaked list as fact. Before you memorize anything, check the status of each claim.

| Claim | Status | What it means for the roster |
|---|---|---|
| Injustice 3 exists | Not officially announced | No confirmed character list exists |
| NetherRealm is the developer | Expected, not confirmed | Same studio behind both prior games |
| Story continues the Injustice 2 ending | Unknown | A reboot or new timeline is equally possible |
| Launch roster size | Unknown | Injustice 2 launched with 28 base fighters |
| DLC fighters planned | Highly likely | Both prior games had 6–9 DLC additions |
| Guest characters included | Highly likely | Every Injustice game has used guests |

A useful rule of thumb: if a source cannot point to an official trailer, a developer statement, or a press release, treat the roster as a rumor. Community reports are fun to read, but they are not confirmation.

### How Big Should the Roster Be?

For context, *Injustice: Gods Among Us* launched with 24 fighters and added 6 DLC characters. *Injustice 2* launched with 28 and added 9 more. Player experience suggests that a launch roster in the high twenties is the sweet spot — big enough for variety, small enough that every character gets proper animation and balancing time.

## How to Build Your Own Injustice 3 Roster

If you want to draft a roster that would actually hold up in a real game, treat it like a design document rather than a popularity contest. This five-step framework works whether you are writing a fan post or evaluating someone else's leak.

| Step | Action | Practical tip |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lock a core of 12 | Trinity plus nine story anchors |
| 2 | Add 8 villains | Every hero needs a rival in the story mode |
| 3 | Add 4 wildcards | Obscure picks keep the meta unpredictable |
| 4 | Reserve 4 guest slots | Do not finalize these early |
| 5 | Audit archetypes | Confirm zoners, grapplers, rushdown, and stance fighters are all present |

### The Archetype Balance Check

Run every roster through this quick checklist before you call it finished:

- **Zoners:** at least four fighters who can control space from range
- **Grapplers:** at least three with command grabs
- **Rushdown:** at least five fast, pressure-heavy characters
- **Stance and utility:** at least two fighters with unusual mechanics
- **Beginner-friendly:** at least three with simple, low-execution gameplans

A roster that fails this test tends to feel stale within weeks, no matter how popular the names on the box are. Player experience across both Injustice titles shows that variety in playstyle matters more to long-term retention than raw star power.

## FAQ: Injustice 3 All Characters

**Has Injustice 3 been officially announced?**
Not at the time of writing. Until Warner Bros. or NetherRealm confirms the game, there is no verified Injustice 3 all characters list. Everything circulating online falls into three buckets: credible rumor, community wishlist, or pure speculation.

**How many characters would Injustice 3 launch with?**
Nobody outside the studio knows. Based on the previous two games, a launch roster in the mid-to-high twenties with a DLC pass of six to nine fighters is the most realistic expectation.

**Will guest characters be included?**
Every Injustice game has included at least one guest fighter, so it is a reasonable assumption. Guests are typically revealed later in the marketing cycle, which means an early "full roster" leak almost never includes them.

**Which newcomers are most requested?**
Beast Boy, Constantine, Static, and Zatanna consistently appear at the top of community polls. Whether that translates into a roster slot depends entirely on how the developers want the meta to play.

**Should I trust leaked roster images?**
Be skeptical. Fan-made mockups are easy to produce and spread quickly. If an image cannot be traced back to an official channel, treat it as entertainment rather than information.

The bottom line: the Injustice 3 all characters conversation is more fun when you know which claims have evidence behind them and which are just hopeful guessing. Keep your expectations flexible, watch official channels, and enjoy the speculation — just do not pre-order based on a rumor.
